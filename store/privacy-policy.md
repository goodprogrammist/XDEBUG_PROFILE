# Privacy Policy — Xdebug Cookies

Last updated: 2026-10-11

The extension does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data.

What the extension stores, only in your browser:
- settings (cookie names and values, IDE, icon colors) in the browser extension storage
  (`storage.sync`; the browser may synchronize it between your devices if you enabled browser sync);
- per-site choices (cookie domain, IDE key for the site) in the local extension storage.

What the extension does:
- sets and removes the Xdebug trigger cookies (XDEBUG_SESSION, XDEBUG_PROFILE, XDEBUG_TRACE
  or the names you configured) for the site you choose;
- reads the address of the current tab only to know which site the cookie belongs to.

The extension makes no network requests of its own and contains no analytics or remote code.

Contact: https://github.com/goodprogrammist/XDEBUG_PROFILE/issues
